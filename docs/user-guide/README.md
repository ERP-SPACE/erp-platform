# User guide

Short guides, one per job. Each takes about five minutes to read and shows the screens you will use most. Print the one for your role and keep it by the desk.

| Guide | For |
|---|---|
| [Owner](owner.md) | The Owner: Today, approvals, credit holds, reports, going live |
| [Sales](sales.md) | Sales managers and executives: orders, proformas, invoices, customers |
| [Warehouse](warehouse.md) | Godown staff: receiving, mapping, picking, returns, labels (`/floor` on a phone) |
| [Accounts](accounts.md) | Accountants: invoices and IRN, receipts, supplier payments, statements, opening balances |
| [Purchase](purchase.md) | Purchase managers: purchase orders, goods receipts, purchase invoices, landed cost |

## Everyone

- **Search anything:** press **Ctrl K** (customer, invoice number, barcode, a page).
- **Keyboard:** **?** shows the shortcuts; **↑ ↓ Enter** move through tables; **Ctrl S** saves a form.
- **"Confirm it's you":** some actions ask for your password again (releasing a credit hold, reversing a receipt, cancelling an invoice, paying commission). That is expected.
- **Numbers:** amounts are in rupees with Indian grouping (₹ 4,82,300.00); negative amounts are in brackets and red; lengths are in metres, widths in inches (44″).
- **Light or dark:** press **Ctrl K** and type "theme".
- **Something wrong?** Note what you did and the time, and tell the person who looks after the ERP. Every change is in the audit log.
